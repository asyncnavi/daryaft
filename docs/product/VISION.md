# Daryaft vision

Find what your organisation knows.

Daryaft means receiving, understanding, or gaining knowledge. The platform should make internal knowledge accessible without surrendering control over documents or access permissions.

People should receive answers grounded in source material, understand when evidence is insufficient, and follow citations to sources they are allowed to read. Self-hosting and API access are first-class requirements.

The intended open-source core and console must remain useful without artificial feature restrictions. Possible future hosted services and support do not change that goal.

Success starts with a reproducible local flow using synthetic documents. Production readiness requires separate evidence for security, reliability, operations, and retrieval quality.
